jagent · personal job-search assistant

Less job-board noise. More time for the right roles.

jagent gathers openings from many places, identifies the roles that fit your profile, and gives you one focused place to decide what to do next.

For job seekers

Your daily workflow

The system does the repetitive searching and sorting. You remain in control of the profile, the shortlist, and every application decision.

1

Describe your target

Add your experience, preferred roles, locations, and deal-breakers.

2

Jobs are collected

Openings arrive from job boards, company systems, feeds, and saved imports.

3

Duplicates disappear

The same role from several sites is combined into one clear listing.

4

Best fits rise up

Eligible roles are ranked against your profile and the strongest matches are explained.

5

You decide

Review the shortlist, then apply, save for later, or skip with a reason.

What you getA smaller, explainable queue of relevant openings—plus a lightweight record of your application decisions.

For technical readers

How the pipeline is assembled

A scheduled Python pipeline fetches and stores jobs, applies deterministic filters and semantic ranking, then enriches the shortlist for a local review dashboard.
